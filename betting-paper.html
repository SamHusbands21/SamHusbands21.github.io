PL Betting Model

Project paper — hyperparameters and the EV threshold are locked on 2014/15–2021/22 walk-forward validation (gross p × odds, mean-fold ROI > 0). Classification and betting metrics use later completed seasons. The live site uses the same hyperparameters refit on all completed seasons.

Premier League Eval window: 2022/23–2025/26 XGBoost + Random Forest ensemble
At a glance
Methodology

A multiclass classifier predicting Premier League match outcomes (Home win / Draw / Away win) for upcoming fixtures. Hyperparameters, the Poisson mix weight, and the EV rule are chosen only on 2014/15–2021/22. Reported metrics below use that locked holdout model on later completed seasons. The live predictions page uses the same hyperparameters refit on every completed season (currently through 2025/26), blended with the same frozen Poisson weight.

1

Elo & xG-Elo ratings

Two parallel Elo systems. A standard result-based Elo (K=20, 75-point home advantage) and an xG-Elo updated using expected-goals share rather than binary results — far less noisy and more reflective of underlying quality.

2

Feature engineering

Elo deltas, rolling points-per-game (last 5 & 10), rolling xG for/against, goals for/against, days of rest, and head-to-head win rate over the last 5 meetings. All features calculated strictly before match kick-off.

3

Tree ensemble

An XGBoost classifier and an isotonic-calibrated Random Forest. Walk-forward time-series cross-validation on 2014/15–2021/22 selects hyperparameters, which are then frozen. The tree probability is an equal-weight average of both calibrated outputs.

4

Poisson xG goal model

Independent Poisson scorelines from pre-match 10-game rolling xG attack and defence, versus a tune-window league mean and home-advantage multiplier. Published 1X2 is (1 − w) × tree + w × Poisson, with w chosen to minimise tune-window out-of-fold log-loss. Cards also show expected goals and the top scorelines. A Dixon–Coles low-score correction is not used yet.

5

Value bet identification

Expected value for flags is gross: model probability × Betfair decimal odds. The threshold and Home/Away vs all-outcomes filter are chosen on 2014/15–2021/22 walk-forward validation folds — the cell with the best mean-fold flat ROI above zero and enough bets. The eval window is never used to pick that hyperparameter. Kelly (capped at 25%) uses Exchange net odds as a conservative stake. Far-horizon books whose implied overround is outside 1.00–1.20 are treated as unavailable.

6

Context features

The trees also see promoted-side flags, a UEFA hangover (last European club match within four days), days of rest from an all-competition calendar (PL + UEFA + FA Cup + EFL Cup), and a count of unavailable FPL first-teamers. Named injuries and expected XIs appear on the card from Fotmob when the unofficial API responds; they are not trained. Rest and form use a repaired PL table that fills football-data holes from Understat.

Limitations & next steps
!

Draw modelling

Published probabilities now mix in Poisson scorelines, which improved eval log-loss and AUC. Modal draw recall is still ~0% — a typical Poisson draw probability of ~25% rarely beats home or away as the single most likely outcome. A Dixon–Coles ρ on 0–0 / 1–0 / 0–1 / 1–1 is the next calibration tweak if low-score draws remain understated.

!

Static odds snapshot

Predictions use a single odds snapshot from the daily pipeline run (~08:00 UTC). The optimal bet-placement window — often closer to kick-off once lineups are confirmed — is not exploited. A going-forward CLV ledger now records odds taken vs football-data Betfair closing prices once those matches settle. Far-horizon MATCH_ODDS books are often empty except for 1.01–1.18 leftovers; those prices are dropped unless implied overround sits between 1.00 and 1.20.

!

Exchange commission

Betfair charges commission only on net winnings in a market, not on stake or losing bets. Football’s usual headline is 5%; My Betfair Rewards can make that 2% (Basic) or 8% (Rewards+). Selection uses gross EV. Applying a flat 5% haircut to every winning payout is what previously wiped every tune-window cell to negative ROI. Kelly still uses net odds as a conservative stake.

!

Sample size

Qualifying bets across the eval window are a modest sample; confidence intervals on ROI remain wide. A single bad variance run can flip a small positive ROI to negative. The eval window now includes every completed season after 2021/22.

!

Player-level data is still coarse

The trees see an FPL first-teamer injury count, not who is missing. Named injuries and expected XIs show on the card when Fotmob answers. Confirmed sheets are rare at the 08:00 UTC run. FPL status history is sparse before 2019/20 (end-of-season archives from then; 2014/15–2018/19 stay 0).

Next steps

A

Confirmed lineups in the model

Expected XIs now appear on the card when Fotmob has them. Turning a confirmed sheet into a trained strength proxy (starter market value, key-player flags) still needs a near-kick-off scrape and historical XIs.

B

Named injury features

The model already uses a coarse FPL unavailable-count. Player-specific flags (top xG contributor out) would need a stable historical injury archive aligned to kick-off, not just the live list.

C

Odds movement ("late money")

The difference between opening and closing odds is one of the strongest known predictors of sharp-money involvement. Tracking line movement in the 24–48 hours before kick-off would encode information about informed bettors that the current features entirely miss.

D

Richer form features

Goal difference trend, shot conversion rates, defensive press intensity (PPDA, available from Understat), and separate home/away form splits within rolling windows. These would better differentiate teams with similar points-per-game records but different underlying quality.

E

Motivation & context

Season stage, points to safety or title, and European fixture congestion all affect team motivation and rotation decisions. A mid-table team with nothing to play for in May behaves very differently from a side fighting relegation — information that aggregate form stats cannot capture.

F

Dixon–Coles correction

The Poisson model treats home and away goals as independent. A one-parameter Dixon–Coles adjustment on the 0–0, 1–0, 0–1, and 1–1 cells, fitted on the tune window, would tighten low-score draw calibration without changing the rest of the stack.
